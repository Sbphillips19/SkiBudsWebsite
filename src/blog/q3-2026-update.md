We're excited to share our Q3 2026 update on the SkiBuds rebuild!

## Waitlist Milestone

We've hit **1,247 riders** on the waitlist — thank you to everyone who's signed up! Each email represents a future SkiBuds member who'll help shape the community from day one.

## What We've Been Building

### Core Features Complete

- **Partner matching** by mountain, ability level (Green/Blue/Black/Backcountry), and riding style
- **Run tracking** with GPS — automatically separates lifts from actual skiing
- **Mountain conditions** — forecasts, resort reports, and community observations in one place
- **Group messaging** with opt-in live location sharing for your crew

### Technical Highlights

- Built with **Expo/React Native** for iOS and Android
- **Firebase** backend for auth, database, and real-time features
- **Offline-first** run tracking — GPS works without cell service
- **PWA support** for the marketing site

## What's Next

1. **TestFlight / Play Console beta** — Waitlist members get access first
2. **Mountain directory** — 200+ North American resorts at launch
3. **Partner integrations** — Shop discounts, club events, trip organizer tools
