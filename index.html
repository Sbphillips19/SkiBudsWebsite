What it does

Built for the day, not the desk

SkiBuds started on the chairlifts in Whistler, from conversations about what riders actually wanted on the mountain. It's being rebuilt around the same idea.

Ride with people at your level

Find riders who ski the same mountains, at the same ability, looking for the same kind of day.

Keep your group together

Message your crew, share photos from the day, and use opt-in location sharing to regroup.

Track your runs

Record each run with GPS. Runs are separated from lift rides, so your vertical reflects what you actually skied.

Know the snow

Forecasts and mountain observations in one place, so you can plan around the weather instead of guessing.

Ride at your level

Matched the way the mountain is marked

You pick where you ride. SkiBuds matches you with people who ride there too — so nobody is waiting at the bottom, and nobody is in over their head.

● Green Learning the mountain. Groomers, easy terrain, no pressure.
■ Blue Comfortable most places. Happy to lap groomers all day.
◆ Black Steeps, bumps, and trees. Looking for people who keep up.
◆◆ Backcountry Out of bounds and earning turns. Ride with a partner, always.
